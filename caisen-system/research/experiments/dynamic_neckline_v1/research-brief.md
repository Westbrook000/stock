# Dynamic neckline research brief

Objective: on the same 100 completed stock accounts in `reports/daily-research/full-unified-v12-20261106-w4-rerun/partial-100-report/sample-selection.json`, exceed v8's 33.7566% trade win rate and 61.3449% equal-initial-capital cumulative return, with a requested win-rate target strictly above 50%. A historical in-sample target is not proof of future performance. Keep cutoff 2026-10-30 for matched archived v8 comparisons; report the cutoff and separately describe any later-data runs. Record all failed and successful trials.

Fixed baseline: 100 independent Backtrader accounts, 100,000 yuan each, 100-share lots, confirmation followed by next-session open, 8% actual-entry stop, 20% target, zero costs/slippage, no maximum hold and final-bar exit. Protection begins after the entry session, as in the existing research strategy. Do not tune exit thresholds to claim the user's recognition objective is met.

New direction from the user supersedes the old rule requiring a neckline and pattern to have the same lifecycle. Pressure boundary candidates can be proposed separately from the pattern; structure pivots must be causally confirmed before a neckline is paired/frozen; a later confirmed pressure point may lead to a revision. Each version records discovery/freeze time, supporting points and validity. Earlier decisions and trades remain immutable. Buy confirmation is evaluated against the revision that is available on the decision date; changes in a line alone must not fabricate a past breakout. A genuinely different newly confirmed line may emit a later signal even when the same base points were used; repeated crossings of the same boundary require separate empirical investigation and must not be silently relabeled as a new boundary. A line must cover structure points; treat full-formation candle coverage as a separate experimental constraint, because the user now expressly permits coverage of structure points.

Suppression is one-way: only an eligible, non-terminal main structure can suppress a strictly nested smaller structure known at that time. Internal/unselected structures and target-reached/invalidated parents cannot suppress. Boundary revisions sharing the same trough anchors are parallel versions of one geometry, not parent/child shapes; each distinct version may produce at most one fresh breakout signal after its own availability date. A boundary update never reuses a crossing that happened before the new version was knowable.

W bottom, inverse head and shoulders, triangle bottom and bull flag remain daily research scope. Preserve two independent pressure tests, 2 ATR pressure-pivot confirmation, 1 ATR breakout buffer, six-month bottom duration, low-position and 20% W/IHS falling-line cap as initial engineering parameters unless an experiment explicitly changes one. Avoid tuning multiple interacting conditions simultaneously before causal functionality and initial attribution are verified.

Experiments must bind code/strategy version, source bars, symbol membership, cutoff, point availability times, price units and fixed costs; compare actual Backtrader account ledgers, including rejected orders. Measure trade count, win rate, portfolio equal-start capital return, max drawdown, profit factor, individual-account distribution and split by era. A sample constructed from previously completed long-history stocks is not representative of ETFs or all listed shares. Rows are not independent and multiple candidate tuning can overfit. Develop on earlier years and report later years separately; previously inspected periods are research data, not independent validation. Never treat future realized profits as a chart-pattern truth label.

A prototype using stored v12 structure events must clearly disclose that it cannot discover structures missed by v12. A qualifying final result requires a new full causal recognizer and Backtrader run for all 100, with frozen parameters and prefix/parallel/accounting audits. Record failures, resource usage, and actual runtime. No production rollout merely because a development backtest improves.
